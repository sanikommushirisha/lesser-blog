    How this works

    The test needs both 31 days in the current year and 183 weighted days. Weighted days are all of this year's days, one third of last year's, and one sixth of the year before.

    For example, 120 days in each of three years comes to 180, which does not meet the test. Staying under 183 days this year is not enough on its own, because earlier days carry over.

    Students on F, J, M or Q status can usually leave exempt days out, within their limits, by filing Form 8843. We work that out from your visa history. Other exclusions exist, such as transit under 24 hours and some medical conditions, so leave those days out of your stays.
