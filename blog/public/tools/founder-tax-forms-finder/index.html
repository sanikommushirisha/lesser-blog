    How this works

    Your filing duty follows how the company is classified, not whether it earned money. Most entities file even in a year with no income.

    Due dates here are for calendar-year filers. Form 7004 extends the time to file, not the time to pay.

    A nonresident cannot hold S corporation shares. Form 5472 applies to a foreign-owned US disregarded entity and to a US corporation that is at least 25% foreign owned, and the penalty for missing it is $25,000.
