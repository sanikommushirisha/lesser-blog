Free tax tools for immigrants in tech

Draft. This tool is under tax review and may change.

Quick checks for RSUs, residency, money from India and more, built on the guides from the Lesser Tax team. They are free, and your result comes by email.

  • RSU Tax PlannerAdd every employer and vest to see what you may owe, your safe harbor target and any Social Security overpaid.
  • US Tax Residency ReportLog your trips and visa history to get your day count, residency start date and required forms.
  • Money from India ReportLog each transfer to see your yearly totals, Form 3520 deadlines and any late penalty.
  • 401(k) Exit PlannerCompare leaving, rolling over, cashing out and spreading a withdrawal before you move back to India.
  • Founder Filing CalendarEnter your company and owners to get your federal forms, due dates and penalty exposure.
  • FBAR and FATCA CheckerSee if your foreign accounts need FBAR or Form 8938.
  • Real Salary CalculatorSee your tech pay after taxes.
