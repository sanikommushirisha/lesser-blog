How this works

A cash-out is ordinary income in one tax year, so it stacks on top of your other income. Before age 59 1/2, an additional 10% applies unless an exception applies. Moving abroad is not an exception.

For a withdrawal before you leave, we use the 2026 single-filer brackets and the $16,100 standard deduction. State tax is left out.

After you become a nonresident, the payer typically withholds, often cited at 30% unless a treaty rate is claimed. That is a prepayment and not the final tax, so we show it on its own.
