    How this works

    We add up your salary, bonus and every vest, then apply the 2026 brackets and standard deduction for your filing status to estimate your federal tax.

    Employers usually withhold 22% on vests, and 37% past $1 million of supplemental wages in a year. We compare that with your tax, and check your prepayments against the underpayment safe harbor.

    With two employers, Social Security withholding can run past the yearly limit. We flag any excess and any Additional Medicare Tax. State tax and credits are not included.
