How this works

The gap is your vest income multiplied by your top bracket minus the 22% withheld. On a $200,000 vest in the 32% bracket, that is $44,000 withheld against about $64,000 owed, a $20,000 gap.

Above $1 million of supplemental wages in a year, the flat rate is 37%. For single filers we work out your top bracket from the 2026 brackets and the $16,100 standard deduction. If your RSUs span two brackets, this estimate can run high.

This covers federal income tax only. It leaves out state tax, Social Security and Medicare, and any other income or deductions on your return.
