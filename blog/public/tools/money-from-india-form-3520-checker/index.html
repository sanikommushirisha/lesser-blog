    How this works

    The line is more than $100,000 in a calendar year, counted per recipient. Gifts from related people add together, so $70,000 from each parent is $140,000. Once you cross the line, the full amount is reported.

    Form 3520 is usually due April 15 of the next year, or October 15 with a Form 4868 extension. The late penalty is 5% of the gift for each month, capped at 25%.

    A gift from a nonresident individual is not income. How the money arrives does not change the rule, and the Indian financial year does not matter here.
